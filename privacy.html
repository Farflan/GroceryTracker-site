Privacy Policy

Grocery Tracker for iPhone and iPad · Effective November 23, 2026

Grocery Tracker keeps everything on your device. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn't send your data anywhere.

What the app stores

The groceries you add, the receipts you scan (a reduced copy of the photo and the items, prices, store, date and currency read from it), the names and categories you correct so it reads the next receipt your way, which groceries you used up before they were likely out (counted as "Used in Time"), and your settings. All of it stays in the app on your device.

For the Home Screen widget, the app also keeps a small file it shares only with that widget: how many groceries are due for a check, and when the others will be. It holds no names, prices or stores, and the widget shows only the count.

How receipts are read

On your device, with Apple's Vision text recognition and, where available, Apple Intelligence's on-device model. Neither the photos nor their text leave your device. The camera and your photo library are used only when you scan or choose a receipt, and only the photos you choose are read.

What we collect

Nothing. The developer receives no data from the app: not your groceries, receipts, photos, usage or device details. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Protection and backups

The app's data is stored with iOS file protection, unreadable while your device is locked. Like all app data, it's included in your device's iCloud or computer backups if you use them; those are kept by you and Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Settings › Delete All Data removes everything from the app at once, the widget's file included. Deleting the app removes it from your device. Copies in backups made earlier remain until those backups are replaced or deleted.

Children

The app isn't directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact
