Grocery Tracker

Keep track of the groceries you have at home by scanning your receipts. Everything stays on your iPhone.

A receipt wasn't read correctly

Every reading is shown for review before anything is added: tap an item to fix its name, amount or price, swipe to delete one, or use + to add one. A flat, well-lit photo with the whole receipt in the frame reads best; a long receipt can be scanned in several pages.

Currency

A receipt is taken to be in your iPhone's currency unless it shows another one (£, €, C$, or a code like CAD). You can change it on the review screen. Finances adds up receipts in your iPhone's currency and says how many in other currencies it left out.

“Might be out” and “Likely out”

These are reminders to check whether you still have something, based on how many days have passed since you bought or confirmed it. They can't tell whether food is safe to eat. Follow the package's storage instructions and official food-safety guidance. You can change the number of days per category in Settings, or per item.

Your data

Nothing leaves your iPhone. To remove everything, use Settings › Delete All Data. See the Privacy Policy.
